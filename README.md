# Market Inventory Tracker

An application to aide small business owners and vendors keep track of their product inventory and profit. Users are able to enter each type of product, the amount they have, the cost for each, how much they are selling it for, and how much they've sold into the tracker and save their information. When they make sales, this application will calculate the profits and keep track of how many items are still left in stock. Additionally, users can view their sale history, total cost of all products, total money made on sales, and total profit made.

## Instructions for End User
- You can run the application by running the MarketUI class.
- You can add Products to Market by clicking on the "Add Product" tab, filling out the name, amount owned, cost, and price of the new product in the correct fields according to requirements and pressing the "Add this product" button.
- You can remove Products from Market by clicking on the "Remove Product" tab, enter the name of the product into the field and pressing the "Remove this product" button.
- You can locate my visual component by clicking on "Load/Save" tab and loading or saving by pressing either the load or save button for the response message and icon to appear and state whether it was successful. There is also a fancy underline under overview information in the "Overview" tab, and I set my JFrame icon to be a market PNG.
- You can load the state of my application by clicking on "Load/Save" tab and pressing the "Load File" button.
- You can save the state of my application by clicking on "Load/Save" tab and pressing the "Save Current File" button.
